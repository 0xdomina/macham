A buyer, not a seller.

Shoppers rarely wait on an email reply, and a support queue that answers hours later sells nothing. Macham answers from the store's own catalog the moment a question arrives, orders the price the store set, and stops for a human before any money moves.

Buyer only. Macham reads a store and places an order at the price that store set. It does not haggle, and there is no seller console.

One key. Your store embeds a single publishable key that names and meters it. It is safe in the browser, and the model keys stay on our server.

Any store. Shopify, WooCommerce and JSON-LD catalogs need no store-side code; the reader picks the lane. A rare backend adds a small adapter.

You approve. Search and compare run on their own. A cart is a proposal, and a person approves it before anything is paid.

Live in a paste, with a key of your own.

Add one line before the closing body tag and hand Macham your store's key. The intelligence runs on our server and is metered per key.

<script src="https://YOUR-MACHAM-HOST/macham.js" data-key="mch_live_..." defer></script> <script> Macham.init({ apiKey: "mch_live_...", tenantId: "my-store", assistUrl: "https://YOUR-MACHAM-HOST", }) </script>

Nothing moves on its own.

The rails are the product. Each guarantee is a step a shopper cannot skip and a store can read back.

A locked total

The approved amount is what Macham asks PayPal to capture, within tolerance.

Approve, then pay

PayPal Orders v2 runs create, approve, authorize, capture. Nothing is captured before a person accepts.

A browser is never proof

A shopper's own claim of success does not count. Only the settled order does.

Settled, then anchored

Every settlement is written to the Rialo audit trail you can read back. If it cannot be verified, the record fails closed.

Your prices, your rules

Macham orders the price the store lists. It does not negotiate, and it never edits your catalog.

Your data stays put

Reads are scoped to your own catalog, and one store's data does not cross to another.
